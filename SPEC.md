# Spec: 

## Goal (one or two sentences)
- a simple website where you can track how much exercise your dog has done per day
- it should have an easy overlook on how much time is spend with physical exercise per day and an graphic overview how the exercise amount was over the last 30 day time span
## Users
- dog owners
## Features (numbered, so prompts can refer to them)
F1: profile of the dog
- you can manually create a profile for your dog which includes name, weight, race, and age of the dog
- it is easily readable
- the design is suitable for fun and leisure activites

F2: exercise intake
- you can track the daily physical exercise amount of your dog

F3: overview of the last 30 days
- the overview is optical stated with a graph and easy readable at first look

## Acceptance criteria (how I know a feature works)
- tracking time is possible
- the overview is shown in a graphic

## Out of scope (do NOT build)
- no medical indications included
- no GPS functions

## Look & feel
- easy readable
- fun and leisure suitable
- smooth and playful design
- main colors in green and orange
